2. PROBLEM STATEMENT & SOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everyday personal productivity is heavily fragmented across disconnected flight/hotel
booking engines, isolated spreadsheet budgets, static note apps, and complex project
trackers. Users waste hours manually bridging the gap between planning and execution.

LifePilot AI unifies smart travel planning, autonomous project roadmaps, and document
intelligence under one central AI orchestration engine. Powered by BharatCode API, it
transforms natural language requests into structured, actionable workflows with
evidence-based citations, 8-category budget optimization, what-if simulations, and
collaborative group decision-making.


3. CORE MODULES & FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[FEATURE 1: SMART TRAVEL PLANNING & GROUP COLLABORATION]
- AI Multi-Day Itinerary Generator:
  Creates personalized daily schedules with morning, afternoon, and evening
  activities, transit times, dining recommendations (breakfast, lunch, dinner),
  accommodation targets, activity costs, and preparation checklists.
  Supports dietary choices (Vegetarian, Vegan, Halal, Jain), transport modes
  (Flights, Trains, Roadtrips, Public Transit), and accessibility requirements.

- Single-Day AI Regenerator:
  Regenerates individual days on-demand with custom AI prompts or 1-click presets:
  * Rain-Safe Indoor Day (museums, galleries, covered markets, cozy cafes)
  * Chill & Relaxed Pace (late brunch, beach relaxation, scenic sunset views)
  * Cultural Immersion (historic colonial architecture, artisan workshops)
  * Budget-Friendly Day (free heritage walks, public gardens, street food)

- Smart 8-Category Budget Optimizer:
  Tracks Transportation, Accommodation, Food, Activities, Shopping, Local Transport,
  Emergency, and Miscellaneous. Features overspending detection, per-person calculations,
  and AI cost-cutting recommendations visualized via interactive Recharts.

- Interactive Travel Map & Route Optimization:
  Category-based place markers (attractions, stays, restaurants), automatic proximity
  clustering to minimize transit times, and 1-click route optimization.

- What-If Trip Simulator (Core Differentiating Feature):
  Models hypothetical scenarios ("Extend to 7 days", "Cut budget to ₹10k/person",
  "What if it rains on Day 2?", "Add 2 friends"). Features side-by-side comparison
  (Original vs Scenario A vs Scenario B) with delta metrics, feasibility scoring,
  risk analysis, 1-click apply, and 1-click restore from automatic backups.

- Context-Aware AI Travel Companion:
  Conversational trip assistant with persistent chat history. Understands the current
  itinerary and proposes structured modifications with change preview approval cards.

- Group Trip Collaboration:
  * Unique 6-character invite codes (e.g. LPT-GOA26) and instant join workflows.
  * Role-Based Access Control (Owner, Editor, Viewer).
  * Group Voting & Polls with duplicate vote prevention and live percentage bars.
  * Splitwise-style Shared Group Expenses with a Greedy Bilateral Settlement
    Engine that minimizes cross-member debt settlement transactions.
  * Collaborative Task Duties & chronological real-time activity audit feed.

[FEATURE 2: ADVANCED AI PERSONAL KNOWLEDGE HUB]
- Document Upload Center:
  Supports PDF, DOCX (via Mammoth), and TXT files with client/server validation,
  upload progress indicators, and automatic chunking/indexing.

- AI Document Summarizer:
  Generates executive summaries, chapter-wise breakdowns, key findings, and
  extracted dates/entities.

- Evidence-Based Single & Multi-Document Q&A:
  Answers queries citing verified source chunks and page numbers. Prevents
  hallucinations and supports cross-document synthesis.

- AI Study Assistant:
  Generates multiple-choice questions (MCQs), flashcards, study notes, structured
  revision schedules, and provides automated grading of user answers (0-100 score).

- Research Paper Analyzer & Knowledge Graph Visualizer:
  Extracts hypotheses, methodology, results, and limitations; visualizes entity nodes
  and relationship edges.

- Knowledge-to-Action Integration:
  Converts extracted document checklist items directly into LifePilot task records
  and extracted project roadmaps into actionable milestone projects.

[FEATURE 3: AI PROJECT ROADMAPS & KANBAN EXECUTION]
- Idea-to-Roadmap Generator:
  Decomposes natural language objectives into milestone timelines, tasks, complexity
  scores, and signoff checklists.

- Interactive Kanban Board:
  Drag-and-drop task workflow (Todo, In Progress, Done) with priority tags,
  deadlines, and category filters.

[FEATURE 4: CENTRAL AI INTENT ORCHESTRATOR]
- Analyzes user natural language input and classifies intent into TRAVEL, PROJECT,
  KNOWLEDGE, or TASK workflows.
- Human-in-the-loop approval mechanism: AI outputs structured change proposals,
  requiring explicit user confirmation before database persistence.

[FEATURE 5: ZERO-CONFIG DESKTOP LAUNCHER & RESILIENCE]
- Zero-CMD Launcher (`l.bat`): Launches backend and frontend completely headlessly
  without terminal popups and directly opens the browser to http://localhost:5173.
- Dual Database Persistence: Operates seamlessly on MongoDB Atlas, with automatic
  failover to a local JSON/SQLite engine for offline demos.


4. TECHNOLOGY STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Frontend:
  - React.js 18 + Vite (ESM)
  - Tailwind CSS + Warm Theme Palette (#feffdf, #ffe79a, #ffa952, #ef5a5a)
  - Zustand (State Management)
  - Recharts (Interactive Budget & Expense Visualizations)
  - Lucide React (Icons)
  - React Router DOM v6
  - Axios (HTTP Client)

Backend:
  - Node.js + Express.js
  - MongoDB + Mongoose (Atlas / Local)
  - LifePilot LocalStore (Offline JSON/SQLite Persistence Engine)
  - JWT Authentication + Bcryptjs Password Hashing
  - Multer (File Upload Handling)
  - Mammoth (.docx extraction) + PDF Text Extraction
  - Express Rate Limiting & Helmet Security Headers

AI Integration:
  - Primary : BharatCode API (Base URL: https://bharatcode.ai/api/model/v1)
  - Fallback: Google Gemini API (gemini-2.5-flash / gemini-3.5-flash-lite)
  - Offline : Deterministic Local Heuristics & Schema Fallbacks


5. REST API ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Authentication:
  POST   /api/auth/register               - Register new user
  POST   /api/auth/login                  - Authenticate and receive JWT
  GET    /api/auth/me                     - Current user profile

Smart Travel & Group Collaboration:
  POST   /api/trips/generate              - Generate AI multi-day itinerary
  GET    /api/trips                       - List user trips
  GET    /api/trips/:id                   - Trip details
  PUT    /api/trips/:id                   - Update trip
  DELETE /api/trips/:id                   - Delete trip
  POST   /api/trips/join                  - Join group trip via invite code
  POST   /api/trips/:id/regenerate-day    - AI regenerate individual day
  GET    /api/trips/:id/budget            - Get 8-category budget
  POST   /api/trips/:id/optimize-budget   - Optimize budget & get alternatives
  POST   /api/trips/:id/scenarios         - Create What-If simulation scenario
  GET    /api/trips/:id/scenarios         - List scenarios
  POST   /api/trips/:id/scenarios/compare - Compare multiple scenarios
  POST   /api/trips/:id/scenarios/:sId/apply - Apply scenario to live itinerary
  POST   /api/trips/:id/scenarios/restore-original - Restore original itinerary
  POST   /api/trips/:id/invitations       - Generate unique invite code
  GET    /api/trips/:id/members           - List trip members
  PUT    /api/trips/:id/members/:mId      - Update member role
  DELETE /api/trips/:id/members/:mId      - Remove member from trip
  POST   /api/trips/:id/polls             - Create group voting poll
  GET    /api/trips/:id/polls             - List polls
  POST   /api/trips/:id/polls/:pId/vote   - Cast vote in poll
  PUT    /api/trips/:id/polls/:pId/close  - Close poll & tally winner
  POST   /api/trips/:id/group-expenses    - Add shared group expense
  GET    /api/trips/:id/group-expenses    - List group expenses
  GET    /api/trips/:id/settlements       - Get bilateral debt settlements
  POST   /api/trips/:id/settlements/settle- Mark debt settled
  POST   /api/trips/:id/tasks             - Create collaborative trip task
  GET    /api/trips/:id/tasks             - List trip tasks
  GET    /api/trips/:id/activity          - Get real-time activity audit feed
  POST   /api/trips/:id/assistant/chat    - AI Companion chat with itinerary context
  GET    /api/trips/:id/assistant/history - AI Companion chat history

Knowledge Hub:
  POST   /api/documents/upload            - Upload PDF, DOCX, or TXT
  GET    /api/documents                   - List uploaded documents
  GET    /api/documents/:id               - Get document with chunks
  POST   /api/documents/:id/summarize     - AI document summary
  POST   /api/documents/:id/ask           - Evidence-based single document Q&A
  POST   /api/documents/multi-ask         - Multi-document intelligence Q&A
  POST   /api/documents/compare           - Multi-document comparison & differences
  POST   /api/documents/:id/study/generate- Generate MCQs, flashcards & study notes
  POST   /api/documents/:id/study/evaluate- Evaluate student answers
  GET    /api/documents/:id/research-analysis - Research paper analysis
  GET    /api/documents/:id/knowledge-graph   - Entity-relationship knowledge graph
  POST   /api/documents/:id/convert-action    - Convert extracted action to task
  POST   /api/documents/:id/convert-project   - Convert extracted roadmap to project

Projects & Tasks:
  POST   /api/projects/generate           - Generate AI project roadmap
  GET    /api/projects                    - List projects
  GET    /api/projects/:id                - Project details & milestones
  POST   /api/tasks                       - Create task
  GET    /api/tasks                       - List tasks with status filter
  PATCH  /api/tasks/:id/status            - Update Kanban task status

Orchestrator:
  POST   /api/ai/chat                     - Central intent classification & routing
  POST   /api/ai/action/approve           - Execute confirmed AI proposals
